# AngkorFresh Cambodia: The Mango Factory Adventure!

_(Explained like you are 10 years old)_

---

### 1. What is the Story Here?

Imagine you run a super popular fruit snack shop in **Phnom Penh, Cambodia** called **AngkorFresh**.
You make delicious dried mangoes and dragon fruit bags.
Right now, you buy fruit, pack it in Cambodia, and sell it. But you have a big idea:

> _"What if we build a brand new fruit-packing factory right next door in **Southern Vietnam**?"_

By building a factory in Vietnam:

- The fruit is fresher because mango farms are right down the street.
- Trucks can deliver snacks to supermarkets in Vietnam and Cambodia much faster.

**The Big Problem:** Building the factory costs **$2,000,000 USD** on Day 1 (Year 0). The big boss (the CFO, Ms. Sok Dara) asks:

> _"If we spend that $2 Million today, will our company in Cambodia actually become richer, or are we going to lose our shirts?"_

---

### 2. The 5 Big Traps You Must Watch Out For

#### Trap 1: "Project Money" vs. "Parent Money" (The Remote Piggy Bank)

Just because the factory in Vietnam makes money does not mean that money automatically appears in your bank account in Cambodia!

- **Project Cash:** Money earned by the factory in Vietnam.
- **Parent Cash:** The actual dollars that make it across the border into Phnom Penh.
  Between the Vietnam factory and your home pocket, there are toll booths:

1. **Local taxes** in Vietnam take a bite.
2. **Fruit stock (Working Capital):** You have to leave cash in Vietnam to buy next month's fresh mangoes.
3. **Remittance rules:** You might only send **80% or 90%** of the cash home each year, leaving some behind for safety.

#### Trap 2: The Currency Converter Roller Coaster (VND to USD)

In Vietnam, people pay you in **Vietnamese Dong (VND)**. But your bosses in Cambodia count their money in **US Dollars (USD)**.

- If today $1 = 25,000$ Dong, and tomorrow $1 = 26,000$ Dong, the Dong got **weaker** (depreciated).
- When you bring your Dong to the bank to buy Dollars, you get **fewer Dollars** than before! Even if sales were great in Vietnam, currency changes can melt your profit.

#### Trap 3: Working Capital (The Locked-Up Coins)

You can't just count Sales minus Costs. To run a fruit factory, you have to:

- Buy crates of mangoes before you sell them (Inventory).
- Let grocery stores pay you 30 days after delivery (Accounts Receivable).
  That means real cash is **frozen** inside the factory walls. You only get that cash back when the project shuts down at the end of Year 5!

#### Trap 4: What if Things Go Wrong? (The 3 Scenarios)

We test three possible futures:

1. **Base Case (Sunny Day):** Everything goes as planned (Sales grow 5%, costs grow 4%).
2. **Downside (Stormy Day):** People buy 10% less, costs rise fast (+6%), and the Dong loses 3% of its value every year. Does the project still make money?
3. **Upside (Party Day):** High sales (+7%), slow costs (+3%), and strong currency.

#### Trap 5: Net Present Value (NPV) & Time Travel for Money

A dollar today is worth more than a dollar 5 years from now (because a dollar today can earn interest in a bank!).

- We use a **Discount Rate (10%)** to shrink future dollars back to what they are worth today.
- If we add up all the future shrunken dollars and subtract the **$2,000,000** we spent on Day 0:
  - If **NPV > 0 (Positive)**: The factory creates new wealth! 🎉
  - If **NPV < 0 (Negative)**: The factory destroys wealth. Walk away! 🛑

### 3. How the AI Financial Assistant Helps

You have a robot sidekick on your computer!

- **What it does well:** It spots missing questions (like _"Hey, did you check the Vietnam corporate tax rate?"_), builds spreadsheet templates, and calculates Downside scenarios in seconds.
- **What it CANNOT do:** It cannot make the final decision. It must never invent fake tax laws, and humans must double-check all its math.
